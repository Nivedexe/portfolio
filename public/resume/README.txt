===================================================================
                       RESUME FOLDER
===================================================================

This is the designated folder for your resume PDF.

TO UPDATE YOUR RESUME:
1. Replace or copy your resume file into this directory as:
   public/resume/resume.pdf

2. When you push to GitHub, the portfolio will automatically 
   serve and download this PDF when visitors click "Download Resume".

Note: The site also supports saving as `public/resume.pdf` as a fallback.
The downloaded file will automatically be saved to the visitor's device 
as "Nived_Krishna_Resume.pdf".
===================================================================
